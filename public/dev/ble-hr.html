Heart Rate service not found after connecting

The band advertises the service, but the connection doesn't list it. The likeliest cause: your phone kept an old copy of the band's service list from before Share heart rate was on (Android keeps one for paired devices).

  1. Turn the phone's Bluetooth off, wait 5 s, turn it on.
  2. In the Google Health app, turn Share heart rate off and on.
  3. Connect again here.
  4. If it still fails: install nRF Connect, connect to the band there, open its menu and tap Refresh device cache, disconnect, then connect here.
  5. Do not unpair or forget the band in Android's Bluetooth settings; that unlinks it from Google Health.
If no readings arrive
  1. In the Google Health app, turn Share heart rate off and on again. The band stops sharing after any app disconnects.
  2. Make sure nothing else is using the shared heart rate (another app, a gym machine, a second phone). Sharing is one connection at a time.
  3. Wear the band snugly: some bands send nothing without skin contact.
  4. Start a workout on the band (or in Google Health), then tap Connect again.
  5. Close Chrome completely, reopen this page, and connect once more.
  6. Still nothing: tap Copy log and send it over.

Calories use Keytel et al. (2005). Heart-rate formulas are least accurate for weight training; treat the number as an estimate. Samples stay on this phone until you download them.
